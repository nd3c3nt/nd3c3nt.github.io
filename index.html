About

I'm a senior software engineer with a career spanning console game development, augmented reality, and real-time tooling. My foundation is in C++ — built over 15 years across shipped PlayStation titles, proprietary engines, and performance-sensitive desktop applications.

At Ready At Dawn Studios, I implemented the full audio pipeline for God of War: Ghost of Sparta on PSP, working directly with sound designers at Sony Santa Monica Studio using Sony's proprietary SCREAM tooling — then ported the entire system to PS3 for the Origins Collection.

Since then I've been at Envisics, building complex C++/Qt desktop tools for cutting-edge holographic AR HUD technology — integrating hardware systems, real-time visualisation, and internal production workflows — with two granted patents to my name.

Away from engineering, I DJ under the alias nd3c3nt, playing drum & bass, deep dubstep, and bass music — which keeps my ear well-tuned and my enthusiasm for audio very much alive.
