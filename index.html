About

I'm a senior software engineer with 15+ years of professional experience across console games, augmented reality, music technology, and real-time systems. My career spans studios in the US and UK — from indie PC games and AAA console titles through to holographic AR HUD research at the cutting edge of automotive display technology.

In games, I've shipped titles on PlayStation, Xbox, Wii, PSP, PC, iOS, and Android across studios including Ready At Dawn, Double Helix Games, Zya/Music Mastermind, and Stratogon. My work on God of War: Ghost of Sparta as audio implementer and technical designer earned a BAFTA nomination. I've worked as a gameplay programmer, audio engineer, tools developer, and technical designer.

Since 2017 I've been at Envisics, building C++/Qt real-time tools for holographic automotive HUDs — integrating eye-tracking, LiDAR, FPGAs, and sensor hardware at R&D depth — with two granted patents from that work.

Away from engineering I DJ as nd3c3nt — drum & bass, deep dubstep, bass music — and run live Twitch AV sessions combining music performance with real-time visuals.
